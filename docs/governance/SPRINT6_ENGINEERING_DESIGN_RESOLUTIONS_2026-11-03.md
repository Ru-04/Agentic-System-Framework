## 2. Test obligations created by these resolutions

- **Replay and concurrency (OPEN-05):** two transactions race to consume one confirmation: exactly one succeeds. Replay after consumption is refused. Expiry is enforced at consume time with `now()`.
- **Trusted identity (section 3 of contract):** a request body that names an operator, role, actor or workspace is refused or ignored; authority comes only from the proof.
- **Fail-closed execution:** with no valid confirmation, a specialist is not invoked. Any missing evidence path yields UNKNOWN, not an error that masks it.
- **Invented citations:** a citation without an identifiable source, or with a digest that does not match, is rejected.
